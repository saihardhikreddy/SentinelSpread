Why WAV & MP4 formats? (MP3 / M4A vs Uncompressed PCM)

MP3 & M4A formats use lossy compression algorithms (psychoacoustic masking and MDCT quantization). This process overwrites least-significant bits during encoding, destroying hidden steganographic data.
WAV (16-bit PCM) & MP4 video containers preserve uncompressed audio samples and frames bit-for-bit, guaranteeing 100% loss-free payload extraction and maximum embedding capacity.
